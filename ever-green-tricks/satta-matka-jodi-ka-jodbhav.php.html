Welcome to our tips and tricks section, today we will learn about the Addition of the Bracket when we had a Jodi. It is our addition of the bracket also called the sum of the Jodi, so today's Jodi is total open plus close equals to next day's Kalyan and Main four-figure.

Today's Jodi Total, Open Plus Close, tomorrow in the Kalyan and Main, there are 4 figures which occur 99% of the time. So, the data you can check out, and understand the example, helps you understand the logic in a better sense.

Here you can see the definition of all the numbers and their meaning. 0 is equal to 2578, 1 is equal to 4569, 2 is equal to 3480, 3 is equal to 2679, 4 is equal to 1689, 5 is equal to 2347, 6 is equal to 2390, 7 is equal to 1456, 8 is equal to 2347, 9 is equal to 1346.

For example, just like in the Kalyan on Friday, 15. If the Jodi is 15, then open plus close, so 1 plus 5, it will become 6. Now 6, what we had in the assignment of the 6? It was 2390. So now, the Jodi will formed from 16.

So that's why in 16 Jodi and 4 figures 2390, they occur most probably on the next day, on the other day like Saturday. So Saturday Kalyan, the Jodi 22 was passed easily.
