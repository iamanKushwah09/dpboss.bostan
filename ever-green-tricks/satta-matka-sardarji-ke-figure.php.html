Satta Matka Sardarji K Figures

Welcome back to dpboss, we are on our evergreen trick section and today we will explore Satta Matka the figures of Sardarji. So according to our experts, Sardarji's figures never fail. On any day, in Kalyan and Main, there are some infallible Anks, so we call them the figures of the SardarJi.

In this trick, we pick out Anks from the card which is very close to it. In this case, what happens is we combine Kalyan's card and find out the Ank for the Main and we combine the Main's card so we can pick out the next day Kalyan Ank from that.

These tricks are very important and very high-performing, According to our authors, these tricks are one of the most accurate tricks you will find in any scheme. So let's suppose your Jodi under the Kalyan is 37. Now according to the mentioned example, we will first add the open card in it.

So it will become 2 plus 3 plus 8 is equal to 13. And similarly, we will add the card of the close in it. For example, 1 plus 7 plus 9 is equal to 17. Now we will sum both of them, 13 plus 17, 30. So here it is a two-figure number, 3 and 0, and if we total them, so it will become 3.

So, this can be said as the final Ank of the Main, 3 and 0. These are called Sardarji K figures, the figures of Sardarji. So there you can see another example and there are tons of examples mentioned above by our experts using the real historical data.
